Our Story

We started with a simple idea: make great things accessible.

What began as a small side project in 2020 has grown into a store trusted by thousands — built on quality, honesty, and a little bit of stubbornness.

Made by people who actually care

We're a small team of designers, makers, and problem-solvers. Every product on this site has been personally tested, debated, and (sometimes) argued about before it makes it to the shelves.

No dropshipping. No filler inventory. Just things we'd genuinely buy ourselves — and quietly judge you for if you didn't.

How we got here

A few milestones along the way.

March 2020

The first listing

A single hoodie, photographed on a kitchen table. It sold out in 3 days — mostly to friends.

October 2021

1,000 orders

Hit our first real milestone. Moved out of the garage and into a proper warehouse.

June 2023

Went carbon-neutral

Switched to eco-friendly packaging, offset all shipping, and started partnering with sustainable suppliers.

Today

Still going

250+ products, 12,000+ happy customers, and the same obsession with quality we started with.

What we believe

Three principles that guide everything we do.

✨

Quality over quantity

We'd rather sell 10 great things than 1,000 forgettable ones. Every product is tested before it's listed.

🌱

Sustainable by default

Eco packaging, carbon-offset shipping, and supplier partnerships that treat people fairly.

🤝

Honest, always

Real reviews, real prices, real people. If we mess up, we'll say so and make it right.
